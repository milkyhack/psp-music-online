# PHASE 4 — Concept Art Pack (Themes 1–15)

**Проект:** Music Online PSP — commercial AAA Sony 2006 exhibition  
**Роль:** Concept Art Team (Sony Design Center / industrial + UI)  
**Дата:** 2026-08-03  
**AD locks:** `design/art_direction/PHASE1_AD_REVIEW.md` (BINDING)  
**Moodboards:** `design/moodboards/PHASE2_MOODBOARDS.md`  
**Wireframes:** `design/wireframes/PHASE3_WIREFRAMES.md` (±4–8 px zone fidelity)  
**NO CODE · NO Phase 5**

Canvas intent: **480×272** PSP feel. Hero paintings generated ~**16:9** as exhibition stills — Implementation crops/scales into wireframe zones.

**Shared finish vocabulary:** brushed metal grain · matte ABS · smoked/tinted glass · acrylic lens edge · soft rubber (TPE) · carbon/textured polymer · anodized aluminum. Specular = prebaked 2–3 stop. Glow = 1–2 additive layers.

# Theme 1 — Sony Walkman Premium

**Exhibition pitch:** Slim EX-era Mg-Al pocket Hi-Fi on the bar — mirror door + amber EL remote jewelry, not fashion NW candy.

**Hero still:** `images/hero_01_walkman_premium.png`

### Hero still (Now Playing) — painter brief

Place a horizontal EX Walkman body as a premium industrial object on a dark exhibition bar surface. **Key** from upper-left (warm booth spot), **fill** cool soft from camera-right, **rim** thin along brushed Al lid and rubber edge. Specular: directional hairline streaks on Al-Mg lid (2–3 stop), hard catch on mirror cassette door. Shadows: deep well under open/closed door recess; soft contact shadow under chassis. **LCD glow:** cool white segment digits under the door illuminate the lower strip only. **EL:** orange/amber jewelry glow confined to left remote stick — never faceplate flood. Materials: mirror door, brushed Al-Mg, matte gunmetal polymer, grey TPE edge, smoked acrylic LCD, plastic remote with EL behind legends.

### Component callouts

| Component | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Transport | Compact rubber key cluster bottom-right under door (`~280,188,144×36`) |
| Panels | Left EL remote strip (`48×180`); main deck body; optional edge volume dial |
| Progress | Thin physical seek under door / in strip — not Material bar |
| Cursor / focus | Subtle key inset press; remote legend brighten |
| Meters | Mega Bass **LED ladder** green→yellow→amber + stereo micro LEDs |

### Motion still frames

1. **Idle:** EL soft breathe (~1 Hz); door mirror quiet; LEDs dark/ghost; segments show clock/stopped.  
2. **Playing peak:** LED ladder climbs; reel shimmer or door window life; EL at mid breathe; title marquee.  
3. **Press:** Transport key 1–2 px inset + specular flatten; brief LED spike.

### Empty + loading

- **Empty:** Mirror door closed/idle; LCD strip “NO TAPE” / “No music”; remote EL dim jewelry.  
- **Loading:** LED ladder climbs as buffer; remote EL breathe; no modal spinner.

### Appearance preview thumbnail

Silhouette must read **door + left remote** at ~252×168 preview size — dual signature, not a silver rectangle.

### Booth self-score: **9/10**

Would fail AD if: pastel pink NW chrome; full-face orange wash; phone FAB; cover-art hero; remote &lt;40 px so EL dies.
